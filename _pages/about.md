What I was saying.. I am an instructional designer and strongly believe that everything is easy once you learn how to do it. 

Growing up in a rural area with a single disabled parent profoundly influenced my _**research trajectory**_ and multiplied my commitment to advancing systemic change in educational practice for underserved students. As a former special educator, consultant to UNICEF Azerbaijan on inclusive education, and trainer in multiple disability-focused professional development projects, my research intersects the design of accessible learning content with learning sciences and the development of advanced learning technologies to improve cognitive and non-cognitive participation of undergraduate students with disabilities in STEM courses in higher education. My theoretical framing draws upon _**emancipatory approaches**_, learning theories, and instructional design practices. I leverage **learning analytics**, **advanced statistical analysis**, and **machine learning** (e.g., t-test, regression, path analysis, equivalence test, NLP, topic modelling) to inform instructional design practices. The goal is to develop evidence-based, scalable instructional models and digitally accessible materials that empower students to learn and faculty to teach STEM in a technology-rich, accessible environment, thereby increasing student success.

To date, I have published in leading journals and venues, including the _Journal of Research on Technology Education_ and _Educational Technology Research and Development_. I have been recognized for my contributions through highly competitive, merit-based scholarships and fellowships and induction into the Honor Society of Phi Kappa Phi (ФКФ), where I served as Student Vice President for two consecutive years. I have been collaborating with and mentoring emerging talent since 2023, aiming to leverage my experience and knowledge to drive meaningful change in the field.
